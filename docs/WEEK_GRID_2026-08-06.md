# CEO Dashboard week grid — investigation & final rule

**Date:** 2026-08-06
**Trigger:** a Case Acceptance entry (Brookvale, 5 Aug) saved fine but never appeared in the
entries table, and a re-encode then hit the duplicate guard.
**Outcome:** three defects found and fixed. One had been hiding **$41,365.56 of July revenue**
from every dashboard view.

---

## 1. TL;DR

| # | Defect | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Case Acceptance page persisted its date filter to `localStorage` with a frozen `to` date, so anything logged after the user last touched the filter was invisible | **Fixed + live** |
| 2 | Search was ANDed with the date range, so a name lookup still only searched the visible window | **Fixed + live** |
| 3 | The days before a month's first Monday were counted by **no month at all** — 11 of 12 months in 2026 had a hole | **Fixed + live** |

Defect 3 is the important one. `getWeekRanges()` started each month on its first Monday and never
crossed the month end, so nothing ever counted e.g. Jul 1–5. The old comment claimed those days
"belong to the previous month's grid", but June's grid stops at Jun 30 — they belonged to nothing.

### What was hidden (2026 YTD, before the fix)

| | Hidden |
|---|---|
| July revenue | **$41,365.56** (Newport $16,815.11, Narrabeen $12,605.42, Brookvale $11,945.03) |
| Case-acceptance entries | 65 |
| Dropouts | 113 |
| Ad spend | $2,523.65 |
| Ad leads | 32 |

July Overall went from **$254,908.91 → $296,274.47** once the missing days were counted.

### 2b. Months with no tab → computed

Jul 2026 onward, and anything before Feb 2025:

- Columns run **Monday → Sunday**.
- **Week 1 starts on the 1st**, unless the 1st is a Saturday or Sunday — then it waits for the
  first Monday. A short Week 1 is fine, and has precedent in her own sheet (May 2025 `[1-4]`).
- Weeks 2–4 follow; whatever is left becomes the **Remainder** (never more than 7 days), `[—]`
  when nothing is left.

| Month | Week 1 | Week 2 | Week 3 | Week 4 | Remainder |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Jul 2026** | **[1-5]** | [6-12] | [13-19] | [20-26] | [27-31] |
| Aug 2026 | [3-9] | [10-16] | [17-23] | [24-30] | [31] |
| Sep 2026 | [1-6] | [7-13] | [14-20] | [21-27] | [28-30] |
| Oct 2026 | [1-4] | [5-11] | [12-18] | [19-25] | [26-31] |
| Nov 2026 | [2-8] | [9-15] | [16-22] | [23-29] | [30] |
| Dec 2026 | [1-6] | [7-13] | [14-20] | [21-27] | [28-31] |

Checked for 2026 and 2027: every weekday sits in exactly one column, no overlaps, always 5
columns. The only days a computed month leaves out are a **Saturday/Sunday that opens the month**
(Feb 1, Mar 1, Aug 1–2, Nov 1 in 2026), because Week 1 waits for the Monday. Those cost nothing —
see §4.

### Monthly Actual

`getMonthRange()` returns day 1 → last day: the **whole calendar month**, regardless of which path
produced the columns. So the Monthly Actual is always complete even when a column leaves a day
out, and for July 2026 `sum(columns) == Monthly Actual` exactly (verified per clinic + Overall).

## 3. Cath's sheets are internally inconsistent

Two sheets were read directly:

- **PhysioWard CEO Dashboard** — `1TVvJP0AksNnuBe4TKJ66NKAWP2jPXn6r30-Zcr2JHZk`,
  owner `catherinephysioward@gmail.com`
- **Brookvale CEO Dashboard** — `1_ES9l4R5jC1HUgrvYJNlr3QDJmzzOcQFn1rcLIEnLhM`,
  owner `physioward@gmail.com`

Both last modified 2026-06-18. **Neither has a July 2026 tab** — they stop at June 2026.

### Watch out: three header rows are not PhysioWard's

The Brookvale file yields 20 week-header rows, but only **17** start with `METRICS`. The other
three start with `ð → Has Formula` and belong to the **original template the sheet was copied
from** — a different business (Wendy/Rex, $1.9M monthly revenue). An early pass in this
investigation mistook those for Nov 2024/Dec 2024/Jan 2025 PhysioWard months, which is where the
odd `[30-5]` and `[27-5]` ranges came from. Exclude them.

### Dating the 17 tabs

Tab titles are lost in the text export, so each tab was dated by matching its Week 1 start against
the calendar. Every one lands on that month's first Monday, or on the 1st, or on a deliberate span
into the previous month — **17 for 17**, so the Feb 2025 → Jun 2026 assignment is certain.

### Four different formats in one file

| Format | Months |
|---|---|
| **Mon–Sun** (7 days) | Feb, Mar, Apr, Sep 2025; **May, Jun 2026** |
| **Mon–Fri** (5 days) | Jun, Aug, Oct, Nov, Dec 2025; **Jan–Apr 2026** |
| Week 1 **starts on the 1st** | May 2025 `[1-4]` (→Sun), Jul 2025 `[1-4]` (→Fri) |
| Week 1 **spans the previous month** | Apr 2025 `[31-6]` = Mar 31 → Apr 6 |

The leading days of a month are handled inconsistently too — parked in the Remainder (Oct 2025,
Jan 2026, Apr 2026) or simply dropped (Feb, Mar, May 2026; Jun, Aug, Nov 2025). The convention
also flip-flopped over time: Mon–Sun through Apr 2025, Mon–Fri from Jun 2025 to Apr 2026, then
back to Mon–Sun in May 2026.

### Her own errors, and what we did with them

| Issue | Handling |
|---|---|
| Apr 2025 Week 1 `[31-6]` includes Mar 31, which March's own Remainder `[31]` also holds → **Mar 31 2025 is counted twice** across the two months | **Preserved** — matching her is the point |
| **Aug 1 2025** and **May 1 2026** (both Fridays) are in no column of her grid | **Preserved** — they are in no column of ours either, but remain inside the Monthly Actual |
| Sep 2025 Remainder reads `[29-31]`, but September has 30 days | **Corrected** — clamped to Sep 29–30 |
| Feb 2025 Remainder is a `[#-#]` placeholder; several others are blank | Treated as empty → renders `[—]` |

## 4. Two measurements worth keeping

### Weekend takings are zero

With Mon–Fri columns deployed briefly, Brookvale April 2026's five columns summed to
**$83,783.47** — identical to the full-calendar-month Monthly Actual. The clinics take no money at
weekends, so a weekend day in or out of a column makes no financial difference. Our DB agrees: zero
case-acceptance rows and zero dropout rows on any Saturday or Sunday in April at Brookvale (front
desk only encodes on weekdays).

### The sheet also disagrees on identical dates

Brookvale April 2026, now that our columns **are** her columns:

| Column | Her sheet | Ours | Diff |
|---|---|---|---|
| Week 1 [6-10] | $16,105.32 | $16,254.80 | +$149.48 |
| Week 2 [13-17] | $24,284.93 | $24,752.26 | +$467.33 |
| Week 3 [20-24] | $16,278.71 | $16,278.71 | — |
| Week 4 [27-30] | $19,141.01 | $19,171.01 | +$30.00 |
| Remainder [1-3] | $7,326.69 | $7,326.69 | — |
| **Total** | **$83,136.66** | **$83,783.47** | **+$646.81** |

Same date ranges, different numbers. Attendance differs too (Week 1: 95 vs 94; monthly 433 vs
428). So **$646.81 of the gap has nothing to do with week format** — it is manual keying, or
payments that landed in Nookal after Cath filled the sheet in. Matching her ranges perfectly, as
we now do, still does not make the two tie out. Do not chase this by changing week logic.

## 5. Deliberately NOT changed

- `getWeekRanges()` keeps its own numbering (Week 1 = first Monday, Mon–Sun, tail Remainder). It
  still backs:
  - **Practitioner Stats** — mirrors Cath's manual weekly KPI sheet, and
    `practitioner_week_inputs.week_num` stores 1–5. Verified still 5 columns showing
    `Week 1 [6-12]` for July.
  - **The weekly Teams notifier** (`scripts/notify-weekly-teams.ts`) — assumes every bucket is a
    full Mon–Sun week.
- **Dropout Analytics** and **Ad Spend** pages: search still respects the date range there,
  because period comparison is the point of those pages.

### Known divergence, in one place only

For **computed** months the dashboard's Week 1 starts on the 1st, so its week numbers are one off
the convention Cath uses (her July Week 1 would be `[6-12]`, which is Week 2 here). Sam was shown
this and chose it. **Transcribed months have no divergence at all.** Practitioner Stats shows
`Week 1 [6-12]` for July while the dashboard shows `Week 2 [6-12]` — intentional.

## 6. Implementation notes

### Backend

| File | Change |
|---|---|
| `services/week.calculator.ts` | `SHEET_GRID` (17 transcribed months) + `computedRanges()` fallback, both behind `getDashboardRanges()`. `getWeekRanges()` untouched. `getMonthRange()` now day 1 → last day (was: first Monday → last day) |
| `services/dashboard.service.ts` | uses `getDashboardRanges()`; `getOverall` pairs per-clinic weeks **by `weekNum`, not array index**; `gridMatches()` cache guard |
| `types/index.ts` | `WeekNum = number \| 'remainder'`; `SheetWeekNum = 1\|2\|3\|4\|'remainder'` keeps the Practitioner Stats path narrow |

### Frontend

| File | Change |
|---|---|
| `Dashboard/DashboardPage.tsx` | `DataRow` takes a `cells[]` array, `SectionHeader` takes `weekCols`; column count follows the payload. It was hardcoded to `wk1..wk4/rem` at indices 0–4, so a payload with a different column count silently shifted every value into the wrong column |
| `CaseAcceptance/CaseAcceptanceEntryPage.tsx` | date range no longer persisted; defaults to the current Mon–Sun week; a back-dated save widens the range so the new row can't hide |
| `Dropouts/DropoutEntryPage.tsx`, `Admin/DropoutAdminPage.tsx`, `Admin/CaseAcceptanceAdminPage.tsx`, `AdLeads/AdLeadsEntryPage.tsx` | a non-empty search drops `date_from`/`date_to` — name lookups span all history |
| `shared/DateRangePicker.tsx` | new **This week** / **Last week** presets (Mon–Sun) |

### Three rules for future work

1. **Never index week columns positionally.** A column's position does not imply a date range —
   the Remainder can hold days from the *start* of the month (Oct 2025, Jan/Apr 2026), and a
   Week 1 can reach into the previous month (Apr 2025). Match on `weekNum`.
2. **Adding a month to `SHEET_GRID`** is how you make a historical month tie out to Cath's sheet.
   Transcribe her header verbatim; don't "fix" her ranges except for impossible dates.
3. **Cache self-heals.** `getMonthly()` refetches whenever a cached snapshot's column dates differ
   from the current grid, so a grid change needs no manual purge. Verified on prod: months cached
   under an older grid returned `fromCache: false` and the new columns on the first plain fetch
   (no `refresh=1`).
